Student Projects and Thesis Work

Student projects and thesis work proposals

Supervising student projects and thesis work is one of the aspects of teaching I value most. It offers an opportunity to work closely with students as they apply their knowledge, develop new skills, and contribute to meaningful research. A research-oriented thesis can also provide an excellent foundation for pursuing a PhD. Possible topics for student projects and theses include: Data-Centric Artificial Intelligence (see this page for additional information) Neuro-Symbolic Artificial Intelligence (see this page for additional information) Information Integration and Ontology-Based Data Management Database Theory and Expressive Power of Formal Languages I am happy to provide background material and discuss possible thesis directions
